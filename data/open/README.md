# Open price datasets

Machine-readable exports of the price tables published on ServerBudget. Each row keeps the official provider URL it was transcribed from, so every figure traces back to its source.

These tables are published alongside the human-readable comparisons at https://serverbudget.com/compare.

## Files

| File | Rows | What it contains |
|---|---|---|
| `vps-plan-pricing.csv` | 9 | Three published tiers at DigitalOcean, OVHcloud and ScalaHosting, with per-GB/per-vCPU unit prices |
| `vps-addon-costs.csv` | 15 | Backup, snapshots and dedicated IPv4 terms as printed on each provider's page |
| `vps-price-vs-renewal.csv` | 3 | Headline price next to the renewal price where one is published |
| `renewal-price-comparison.csv` | 10 | First-term vs renewal at eight providers, with the exact source wording |
| `coupon-offers.csv` | 133 | Promo offers across 26 provider pages, with conditions and official source URL |
| `datacenter-region-pricing.csv` | 6 | Entry to 8-vCPU tier prices by datacenter city |
| `contract-term-total-cost.csv` | 4 | What a 1/12/24/36-month term actually costs |
| `entry-tier-pricing.csv` | 4 | Cheapest tier, hourly and monthly side by side |

For columns, units, source URLs and method for each dataset, see [DATA_DICTIONARY.md](DATA_DICTIONARY.md).

## Method

Generated by `export_open_data.py` from the site's rendered public HTML. Nothing is estimated, converted or averaged; where the source says a value is not published, the cell says so. `manifest.json` records row counts, byte counts and md5 for each file.
